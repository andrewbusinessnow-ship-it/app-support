I deleted photos but my storage didn't go down

iOS keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and they still use storage until then. To get the space back now, open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted > Delete All.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Keep or Toss > Cancel.

I subscribed but still see limits

Open Keep or Toss > Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Photos I kept came back

Settings > "Show kept photos again" brings them back on purpose; otherwise kept photos stay hidden from the deck.

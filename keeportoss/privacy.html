Privacy Policy

Keep or Toss: Photo Cleaner · Effective November 17, 2026

Keep or Toss reads your photo library only on your iPhone so you can review and delete items. Photos, videos and their details are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone.

What is stored

On your device only: the list of photos you chose to keep, your swipe counts, and how much space you've freed. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Deleting photos

Nothing is deleted until you confirm in the standard iOS prompt. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect any personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

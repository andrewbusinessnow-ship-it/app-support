Emailing says Mail isn't set up

Sign in to the Apple Mail app, or use "Share or print PDF" to send through Gmail, Outlook or Messages.

How do payment reminders work?

Turn on "Remind client on due date" on an invoice. On the due date, Billbook sends you a notification; tap it to open a ready-to-send reminder email in Mail. Billbook never emails your customers by itself. Pro adds follow-up alerts 1 day before and 3 and 7 days after the due date.

What is free?

You can create 5 invoices or estimates in total for free. Billbook Pro (monthly or yearly) unlocks unlimited documents, follow-up reminder alerts, an editable reminder email and CSV export.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Cancel.

I subscribed but still see the paywall

Settings in the app > Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. There is no separate cloud sync in version 1.0.

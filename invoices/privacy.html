Privacy Policy

Billbook: Invoice Maker · Effective November 17, 2026

Your business profile, customers, estimates and invoices are stored only on your iPhone. We do not run a server and never receive this information.

Email

When you email an invoice, it is sent through your own Mail app and email account. We don't see or store those messages.

Reminders

If you turn on payment reminders, the app schedules notifications on your iPhone only. Nothing is sent to us or to your customers. A reminder email is only sent if you send it yourself from your Mail app.

Your logo

If you add a logo, you choose one image with the system photo picker. The app never sees the rest of your photo library, and the logo is stored only on your device.

Customer information you enter

You are responsible for the customer details you choose to store. They stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app.

Backups

"Export all data" creates a JSON backup file that you save or share wherever you choose; importing reads that file on your device. We never receive it.

Payments

The app does not process payments. "Record payment" only records what your customer paid you. If you add Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, PayPal.me or check details, they are printed on your invoices, and the QR codes simply link to those services. The app never contacts them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect any personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Privacy Policy

Quote to Paid: Invoice Maker · Effective November 17, 2026

Your business profile, customers, estimates and invoices are stored only on your iPhone. We do not run a server and never receive this information.

Email

When you email an invoice, it is sent through your own Mail app and email account. We don't see or store those messages.

Customer information you enter

You are responsible for the customer details you choose to store. They stay on your device and are removed if you delete the app.

Payments

Version 1.0 does not process card payments. "Mark as paid" only records how your customer paid you.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Analytics and tracking

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect any personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
